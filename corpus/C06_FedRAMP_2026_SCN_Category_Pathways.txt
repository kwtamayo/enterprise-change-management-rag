CURATED INGESTION EXTRACT

Adaptive significant changes require notification to necessary parties within 10 business days after completion, including newly identified risks or vulnerabilities when applicable.

Routine recurring significant changes generally should not receive formal Significant Change Notifications because that category is exempt from formal notification requirements.

Transformative significant changes have staged notification requirements: initial plans at least 30 business days before starting, final plans at least 10 business days before starting, a completion update within 5 business days after finishing, and a verification/assessment/validation update within 5 business days after that work is completed. Updated service documentation and related materials must be published within 30 business days after finishing the transformative change.

A certification-class change is not handled through the ordinary SCN category pathways and requires a new assessment.
